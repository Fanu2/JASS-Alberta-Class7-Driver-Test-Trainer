## 4. What may a Class 5 holder not operate as an ordinary Class 5 driver?
**Answer:** A motorcycle unless learning, a vehicle seating more than 15 while transporting anyone other than the driver, a vehicle transporting passengers for hire, or an air-brake-equipped vehicle unless the supervisor has the required air-brake endorsement.
**Explanation:** Passenger questions can depend on seating capacity and whether passengers are being transported for hire. Do not treat every passenger vehicle the same.
**🧠 Memory trick:** PASSENGERS = CAPACITY + PURPOSE
**Source question:** #46 • 1. Licensing Information • pp. 10–20

## 5. When may a driver turn right on a red light?

**🧠 Memory trick:** YIELD = GIVE WAY

## 7. What special rule applies to parking on a primary highway outside an urban area?
